Today, we’re excited to share Enact, a research preview of scenes that respond to you. Enact represents a new way of making AI video, one that’s designed to mimic the experience of directing on set. Built on Pika H3 Flex, it allows a creator to interact with “actors,” manually re-stage a scene, and manipulate the camera—all in real time.
